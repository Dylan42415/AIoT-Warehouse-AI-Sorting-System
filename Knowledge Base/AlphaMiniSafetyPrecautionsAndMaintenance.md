# Alpha Mini Safety Precautions and Maintenance

The **Alpha Mini Robot** is an educational humanoid precision robotics platform valued at over S$1,000 per unit. To prevent mechanical damage, servo stripping, battery hazard, and personal injury, strict handling protocols must be observed.

---

## Mandatory Do's and Don'ts

### The "Do's"
1. **Proper Lifting Grip:** Always pick up the robot by grasping the **handy grips** on the upper rear backpack. Never lift the robot by its head, arms, or legs.
2. **Proper Charging Pose:** When charging while in sleep mode or powered off, place Alpha Mini in a stable **kneeling or sitting forward pose** on a flat table surface.
3. **Flat Hard Surfaces:** Operate the robot exclusively on smooth, rigid, horizontal surfaces with ample clearance.
4. **20 cm Safety Radius:** Maintain at least **20 cm of clear space** above, in front, behind, and on both sides of the robot during motion execution.
5. **Pre-handling Assessment:** All students/operators must read the user manual and pass the safety quiz with a minimum score of **75%** before handling the robot.

### The "Don'ts"
1. **No Pinching / Touching During Motion:** Never touch, grasp, or impede the robot while joints are actively moving to prevent pinch injuries and servo gear stripping.
2. **No Table Edge Placement:** Never place the robot near the edge of an elevated table or desk where dynamic motion could cause it to fall.
3. **No Joint Torquing:** Never forcefully twist, pull, or bend the movable limb or head joints by hand. The internal servo gearboxes are precision engineered and will shear under forced manual deflection.
4. **No Inclined Surfaces:** Never place or run Alpha Mini on slopes, ramps, or uneven substrates.
5. **No Dropping or Smashing:** Avoid impacts, drops, or throwing the robot.
6. **No Non-Official Upgrades or Disassembly:** Do not disassemble, modify, or attempt self-repair. Contact `aftersales@ubtrobot.com`.

## Environmental & Battery Maintenance Protocols

### Storage & Cleaning
- **Storage Environment:** Store in a dry, well-ventilated, clean environment away from moisture, dust, and direct sunlight.
- **Storage Box:** Always return the robot to its designated custom Styrofoam storage box along with the official cable and power adapter.
- **Regular Refresh Charging:** When kept in long-term storage, charge the robot **at least once a month** to prevent irreversible lithium cell degradation.
- **Cleaning Protocol:** Turn off and disconnect power before cleaning. Wipe surface using a damp microfiber towel with a small amount of neutral detergent. Dry naturally before operating.

### Battery Life Warning Signs
The robot utilizes an internal 4060 mAh 3.85 V lithium polymer cell. The battery is considered **exhausted / defective** if:
1. It fails to reach **80% capacity** after repeated charging cycles; OR
2. It exhausts **100% of its stored power within 15 minutes** of operation.
*Action:* Cease charging immediately and contact official support (`aftersales@ubtrobot.com`).

## Complete 16-Point Verbatim Warning Catalog

As specified in the official UBTECH User Manual and EU Declaration of Conformity:
1. The packaging must be retained since it contains important information.
2. The battery charger used with the toy is to be regularly examined for damage to the cord, plug, enclosure, and other parts; in the event of damage, do not use until repaired.
3. Supply terminals, battery terminals, and connectors must not be short-circuited.
4. Misuse of transformer can cause electrical shock.
5. Use only recommended transformer (rated DC 5V 3A / 9V 2A / 12V 1.5A). The transformer is not a toy.
6. Choking hazard: small parts inside. Not intended for children under 3 years old.
7. Disconnect from transformer before cleaning.
8. Transformers and power supplies for toys are not intended to be used as toys; children must use under full parental supervision.
9. Beware of sharp points on the end of the SIM ejector pin.
10. Battery packs must not be exposed to excessive heat such as sunshine or fire. Follow environmental battery disposal rules.
11. Only use attachments/accessories specified or provided by the manufacturer.
12. Do not touch when moving to avoid hand injuries.
13. Disposal of battery into fire, hot oven, or mechanical crushing/cutting can result in explosion.
14. Leaving battery in extremely high temperature environments can result in explosion or flammable liquid/gas leakage.
15. Battery subjected to extremely low air pressure may result in explosion or flammable leakage.
16. Safe distance is **20 cm or more** during operation.
